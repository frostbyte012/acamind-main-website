About

Acamind, established in the year 2019, is an online learning platform where students are guided to qualify for various competitive examinations like GPAT, NIPER, GATE, WBJEE as well as boards in a very simple and friendly manner. Acamind looks forward to providing proper mentorship (career counseling, skill development, mental health, and study materials) through a family-like environment. Acamind also provides basic to advanced knowledge regarding coding (C++, Java, Python), industrial and manufacturing units, lab instrument handling techniques, documentation, etc. Our major goal is “To stay together and grow together”.
